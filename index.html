Behind the Canvas

Hi, I'm Alex. I'm an illustrator based in the Pacific Northwest, drawing inspiration from nature, mid-century modern design, and everyday absurdities.

With over 8 years of experience in the creative industry, I've had the pleasure of working with global brands and local startups alike. My work combines traditional ink techniques with modern digital tools to create textures that feel warm and authentic.

When I'm not drawing, you can find me hiking the Cascades, brewing overly complicated coffee, or trying to stop my cat from walking across my tablet.
